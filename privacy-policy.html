Privacy Policy

How Broad Solution Tech collects, uses, and protects your information.

Last Updated: May 2026  |  Broad Solution Tech  |  Lahore, Pakistan

1. Introduction

Broad Solution Tech ("BST", "we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website broadsolutiontech.com or engage our services.

By using our website or services, you consent to the data practices described in this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use our platform.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information you voluntarily provide to us when you:

  • Submit a contact or project inquiry form
  • Send us an email at hello@broadsolutiontech.com
  • Reach out via WhatsApp or social media
  • Subscribe to our newsletter

The information collected may include your name, email address, company name, phone number, and project details. We also collect standard web server logs (IP addresses, browser type, referring pages) via analytics.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect exclusively to:

  • Respond to your inquiries and project requests
  • Send project proposals, agreements, and updates
  • Improve our website and services based on usage patterns
  • Send occasional newsletters (only with explicit consent)
  • Comply with legal obligations

We do not sell, rent, or lease your personal information to third parties for marketing purposes.

4. Information Sharing

We may share your information with:

  • Service Providers: Trusted third-party tools we use to operate (e.g., Formspree for form handling, Vercel for hosting). These parties are contractually bound to keep your data confidential.
  • Legal Requirements: When required by applicable law, court order, or governmental regulation.

We will never sell your personal data. Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreements (NDAs) are available upon request for all client engagements.

5. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organisational security measures to protect your personal information. However, no transmission of data over the internet is guaranteed to be completely secure. We encourage you to use secure channels for sensitive information.

6. Cookies

Our website may use essential cookies to maintain session state and improve user experience. We do not use third-party advertising cookies. You can configure your browser to refuse cookies, though this may affect site functionality.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction or deletion of your personal data
  • Withdraw consent for marketing communications at any time
  • Lodge a complaint with a data protection authority

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at hello@broadsolutiontech.com

8. Policy Updates

We reserve the right to update this Privacy Policy at any time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date. Your continued use of our services after any changes indicates your acceptance of the updated policy.
